## What this plugin actually does (capability catalog)

It is a **bridge**, not a job board. It owns almost no rendering of its own: it takes
WP Job Manager (plus its Resumes / Applications / Alerts / Bookmarks add-ons) and surfaces
them **inside BuddyPress member profiles**, so a member manages jobs and resumes without
leaving their profile.

| Capability | Owned by | Surface |
|---|---|---|
| Post a job from your profile | WPJM form, our tab | Jobs → Post Job |
| Manage your job listings | WPJM `[job_dashboard]`, our tab | Jobs → My Jobs |
| Read-only job list for non-posters | **ours** | Jobs (bare) |
| Job alerts | Alerts add-on, our tab | Jobs → Job Alerts |
| Bookmarked jobs | Bookmarks add-on, our tab | Jobs → My Bookmarks |
| Manage resumes | **our template** | Resumes → My Resumes |
| Add a resume | Resume Manager form, our tab | Resumes → Add Resume |
| See jobs you applied to | **our template** | Resumes → Applied Jobs |
| Apply to a job | WPJM `[job_apply]`, **our page + template** | `/apply-to-job/?args=<id>` |
| Show latest resume on profile | **ours** | Profile → after profile loop |
| Resume field visibility | **ours** | Profile → Resume Fields |
| Notify job author of an application | **ours** | BP notification |
| Job/resume activity posts | **ours** | BP activity stream |
| Settings | **ours** | Admin, 4 tabs |

Data model: **no tables, no CPTs of its own.** It operates entirely on WPJM's
`job_listing` / `resume` / `job_application` posts and BP activity. One option
(`bpjm_general_settings`), one AJAX action (`bpjm_load_more_jobs`), zero REST routes.

**Consequence that drives every finding below:** most pixels on our screens are third-party
markup rendered in our frame. We own the *frame, the context and the coherence* — so "it's
WPJM's markup" is not a defence when the screen reads as broken to a member.

## Findings — what is not presentable

Ranked by what a customer would complain about first.

### 1. Non-posters get an employer's empty dashboard — `F5` · ours · high

A member whose role cannot post jobs still gets a **Jobs** nav item. Opening it shows a
screen headed **"My Jobs"** with an employer-oriented empty table — `TITLE / FILLED? /
DATE POSTED / LISTING EXPIRES / ACTIONS` — and "No jobs found." **There is no subnav row
at all**, so the screen also looks unfinished next to the poster's 4-tab version.

A candidate can never have jobs. This is a whole nav item and screen that can never do
anything for the majority role on a job site. Either it should not appear for them, or it
should show what they *can* do (browse jobs / their applications).

### 2. Applied Jobs never shows application status — `F9` · ours · high

The one thing a candidate wants from this screen is "what happened to my application?"
The table is `Applied For | Applied Date | Attachment`. There is **no status column**,
though WPJM Applications tracks status per application. A member applies and the screen
can only confirm the application exists.

Also: all three headers are centre-aligned while the first column's values are
left-aligned, so "Applied For" floats off-centre above its own column.

### 3. "Sign out" button inside the Post Job form — `F2` · fixable by us · high

On the member's **own** profile tab, WPJM renders its account block:
_"You are currently signed in as **bpjm-employer**"_ with a **Sign out** button, mid-form.
Three problems: it is redundant (they are on their own profile), it prints the raw
**username** rather than the display name, and it places a destructive action in the middle
of a form being filled in — one mis-click loses the draft.

We deliberately do not filter `submit_job_form_show_signin` because a logged-out visitor
needs the sign-in box (that suppression was a past bug). The fix is narrower: suppress it
only when the viewer is logged in and on their own profile.

### 4. Empty states are bare table rows — `F4`, `F5` · high-ish

`My Bookmarks` renders a table with headers and the sentence "You currently have no
bookmarks". `Job Alerts` centres "No job alerts found." in a large empty box. Neither has
icon + title + guidance + CTA. These are the **first** screens a new member sees, and they
read as broken rather than empty.

### 5. Button vocabulary is inconsistent across our own tabs — `F2`/`F3` · medium

Post Job uses solid blue primaries (Preview, Save Draft). Job Alerts' only CTA is a
full-width **outlined** "Add alert". Same plugin, adjacent tabs, two different button
languages — because each is a different add-on's markup and we impose no shared vocabulary.

### 6. Standards drift (§1 grep) · 1 block + 18 advisory

| Severity | Rule | Location |
|---|---|---|
| **block** | inline `<style>` in PHP | `admin/class-bp-job-manager-admin-feedback.php:183` |
| advisory | Dashicons instead of Lucide | 10 sites (admin panel, hub, discover) |
| advisory | `outline: none` with no `:focus-visible` replacement | `assets/css/admin.css` ×4 — keyboard focus invisible |
| advisory | 7 distinct breakpoints (standard is 3) | global |
| advisory | raw `margin-left` / `padding-right` (RTL-fragile) | `assets/css/admin.css` ×3 |

The `outline: none` rows matter most — four admin controls lose their visible focus ring,
which is a keyboard-accessibility regression, not cosmetics.
