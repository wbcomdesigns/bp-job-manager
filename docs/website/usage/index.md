# Usage

Common tasks, from both sides of the hiring journey. Every screen below is rendered by WP
Job Manager or one of its add-ons; this plugin only frames it inside the profile.

## Post a job

1. Open your own profile and click the **Jobs** tab.
2. Go to **Post Job** and fill in WP Job Manager's submission form.
3. Submit. The listing now appears under **My Jobs** on your profile.

Who may post is decided by WP Job Manager, not by this plugin. If posting is denied, the
member simply does not see the Post Job form.

## Manage and track your jobs

- **My Jobs** lists the jobs you posted, with WP Job Manager's own edit and status controls.
- Long histories load more entries on demand, so the dashboard stays fast.

## Add a resume

Needs the Resume Manager add-on.

1. Open your profile and click the **Resumes** tab.
2. Go to **Add Resume** and complete Resume Manager's form.
3. The resume now shows under **My Resumes** on your profile. You can keep more than one.

## Apply for a job and track it

1. Open a job listing on the site and apply there. Any logged-in member can apply; that is
   WP Job Manager's behaviour, and the profile matches it.
2. With the Applications add-on active, open your profile, go to the **Resumes** tab, then
   **Applied Jobs**.
3. Each application is listed with the status the employer set.

## Get notified when someone applies

With the Applications add-on active, the job author receives a BuddyPress notification for
each application. Turn it off under **Application notifications** in the General settings if
you do not want it.

## Message a candidate

When the BuddyPress Messages component is active, a candidate's contact details carry a
**Send Private Message** link that opens a BuddyPress private message to that member.

## Publish jobs and resumes to the activity feed

New jobs and new resumes can each post to the BuddyPress activity stream. Both are toggles in
the General settings. When a job or resume is later trashed or deleted, its activity entry is
removed automatically.

## Save jobs and manage alerts

- With the Bookmarks add-on, saved jobs appear on the **My Bookmarks** tab.
- With the Job Alerts add-on, members manage their alerts on the **Job Alerts** tab, without
  leaving their profile.
