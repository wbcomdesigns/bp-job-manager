# Resumes and applications

This page covers the candidate side: resumes, applying and following applications. The Resumes tab needs the Resume Manager add-on. Applied Jobs needs the Applications add-on.

## Resumes on a member profile

With Resume Manager active, a member's own profile has a **Resumes** tab:

| Screen | What it does |
|---|---|
| My Resumes | Resume Manager's candidate dashboard: the member's resumes and their actions |
| Add Resume | Resume Manager's resume form |

### Add and manage a resume

1. Open your profile and go to **Resumes > Add Resume**.
2. Fill in the form and submit it.
3. Open **Resumes > My Resumes** to see, hide, publish, edit or delete it.

- **Relist** on an expired resume opens **Add Resume** with that resume loaded.
- Saving an edited resume keeps the member on their profile, even when Resume Manager's Candidate Dashboard page is set.
- A member can keep several resumes.

### What visitors see

- Another member's **Resumes** tab appears only when that member has at least one published resume and Resume Manager allows the visitor to browse resumes. A visitor who may not browse resumes does not see the tab.
- The tab shows the member's published resumes as resume cards, with page links.
- When the Messages component is active, a **Send Private Message** link is added to a resume's contact details. It opens a new BuddyPress message to the resume's owner.

The resume is not repeated on the main profile screen. It lives only on the Resumes tab.

## Apply for a job

Applying stays with the Applications add-on. The member opens a job and uses the apply form it provides. This plugin adds the screens and notifications around it.

### Follow your applications

1. Open your profile and go to **Jobs > Applied Jobs**.
2. Read the list of your applications and their status.

The old address under Resumes redirects to **Jobs > Applied Jobs**.

### What the employer does

1. The employer opens **Jobs > My Jobs** on their profile.
2. They choose **Show applications** on the job.
3. They change an application's status with the Applications add-on's controls.

With **Application notifications** on, the employer is notified of each new application and the candidate is notified of each status change. See [Activity and notifications](activity-and-notifications.md).

## Bookmarks and alerts

With the Bookmarks add-on, **Jobs > My Bookmarks** lists the jobs a member saved. With the Job Alerts add-on, **Jobs > Job Alerts** lets the member manage their alerts. Both appear only on the member's own profile.

## Toolbar menu

With Resume Manager active, logged-in members get a **Resumes** menu in the toolbar with **My Resumes** and **Add Resume**, and their resume count.
